The workspace edit path `saveCatalogProductWorkspace` in `catalog.ts` is another concrete bypass: it directly updates ACTIVE price definitions (amount, compare-at and currency) and inserts when absent, around lines5851–5867. Approved enforcement must cover this update branch as well as creation and the Pricing service. Its current direct-update behavior is source evidence, not a recommendation to normalize historical records in place. The API Catalog schemas accept four-place decimal strings; they do not establish integer-only authority for direct domain callers.

Normalization requires a separately approved inventory of current definitions and tariffs, proposed new prices/effective dates, revised compare-at relationships, impact on promotions/manual overrides, and quote invalidation. Use new effective definitions/revisions and normal migrations for schema changes; do not edit historical migrations, Orders, receipts or audit history. The read-only fractional audit is an inventory aid, not a mutation or approval mechanism.
