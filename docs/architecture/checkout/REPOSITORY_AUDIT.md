## Replacement matrix

**Reuse:** Orders identity/lifecycle/snapshots; money conventions; Inventory reservations and release; Pricing/Promotions; geography/rates; Payments/Finance allocations and settlement; Customer restrictions; IAM; outbox/Worker; authored Storefront and Admin primitives; Cart authority and revision patterns.

**Refactor:** Checkout application boundary out of Orders transport/persistence; placement into orchestration plus transaction-aware domain operations; Payments into methods/destinations, claims/review, receipts/allocations, refunds, COD and queries; legacy DTOs into shared bounded contracts. Preserve exports only during an inventoried transition.

**Replace:** demo Checkout hook and fixtures in runtime, client-derived payable totals, synthetic confirmation, hard-coded destinations and delivery promises, single-current-cookie-only recovery, guest-Cart-only initiation, weak tracking lookup and old process-local quotas on sensitive purchase flows.

**Migrate:** Checkout source/owner, quote revisions, destination snapshots and receipt identities; existing active sessions classified as legacy, never falsely attributed to an account. Preserve all Order/Payment/Finance/Inventory foreign keys, documents, receipts, audit and outbox IDs. Historical-data quality is NOT VERIFIED without an authorized target inventory.

**Remove later:** unreachable obsolete demo runtime and legacy route consumers after code/import/ingress inventory and compatibility tests prove retirement. Keep any useful design showcase under development-only fixtures. Do not remove old migrations or historical order columns in this project merely to simplify code.

**Temporarily preserve:** legacy confirmation/payment support for already-placed Orders through a hardened scoped adapter; Admin/manual-order and courier/settlement contracts; expired-session support recovery where ownership is proven. Expired unplaced drafts can be restarted without deleting historical evidence.
