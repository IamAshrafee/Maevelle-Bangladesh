Research date: 2026-11-04, Asia/Dhaka. Links are official primary sources. This is architectural research, not legal advice, merchant-account approval or an exhaustive finding that no later law/circular applies. Recheck current provider/official requirements at implementation and activation. Recommendations in this package are engineering choices unless explicitly attributed below.

## Activation checklist owned outside Phase 00

Legal/merchant owner must record applicable current instruments and exact clause interpretation: business/DBID/trade/tax registrations, honest product/price/stock/delivery disclosures, complaint/contact accessibility, advance/escrow collection model, cancellation/refund deadlines and payment evidence/retention. Accountant approves tax/fee/rounding/invoice policy. Providers approve receiving-account type, numbers, public name/instructions, transfer-reference uniqueness and statement verification/refund process. Privacy owner approves saved-address/history linkage, recovery channel and analytics exports/retention. Evidence should record approver, date, agreement/circular version and affected policy IDs D01–D08, without credentials or customer records.

These unresolved external gates do not prevent completing an engineering blueprint. They do prevent representing manual payment activation or legal compliance as accepted. Production collection stays disabled where required approval is absent; COD also requires its consumer/operational policy acceptance.
