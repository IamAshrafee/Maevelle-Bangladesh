## Existing boundary

The shared Checkout controller now calls quote create/current/detail/acknowledgement with exact string root revision, owned draft and quote fingerprint. Creation advances the shared root once; acknowledgement does not. Unknown writes retry the same private in-memory key/body. Immutable facts and predecessor history are distinct from current eligibility observations. Both sources and Cart-owned coupons are supported. Delivery's old `quoteAvailable:false` remains intact; the new quote surface uses its own exact contract.

`evaluatedTotal` is merchandise minus discounts plus evaluated shipping. It is not a payment obligation. `tax:null`, `paymentFees:null`, `payable:null`, `final:false`, `stockReserved:false`, `purchaseAvailable:false` remain mandatory. Acknowledgement of components never resolves TAX_POLICY_UNRESOLVED, PAYMENT_POLICY_UNRESOLVED or COURIER_COMPATIBILITY_UNVERIFIED. No quote ID, fingerprint, acknowledgement, coupon entry or client total authorizes funds, allocation or Order placement.

## Required policy and authoritative capabilities

1. Owner/accountant decisions: tax-inclusive/exclusive treatment, applicable rates/exemptions/invoice requirements, payment fees/waivers, approved two-place BDT rounding and explicit rounding adjustment, and resulting payable/obligation reconciliation. Preserve four-place component precision; the frontend must never choose these policies.
2. Payment-method eligibility: real COD/manual wallet/provider availability, amount/currency/geography limits, fee and courier compatibility, method-specific workflow, approved payment and review deadlines. Define versioned evaluated method conditions and revalidation; no hardcoded universally available methods.
3. Receiving destinations belong to existing Payments settings and secret/integration infrastructure. Require merchant-confirmed provider/account type, recipient identity/number, currency, public instructions, immutable revision, activation/suspension/retirement, capability authorization and audit. Apply proposed strong Owner reauthentication/approval controls from PAYMENTS_AND_BUSINESS_RULES; do not equate login with authorization. Separately authorize any live verification. Destination compromise must suspend new use, preserve pending historical instructions/evidence and support explicit remediation. No personal environment literals or shopper-supplied recipient can become merchant authority.
4. Extend current shared contracts/semantic operations for final commercial/payment evaluation after policy approval. Expose honest readiness/reasons and version binding; never reinterpret nullable quote fields as zero. Re-evaluate price/promotion/shipping/delivery/source/address/method/destination and compare exact root, quote and accepted fingerprint. Component acknowledgement may need renewed explicit review after any final payable/method/destination change.
5. Future placement must reuse Orders, Inventory, Promotions, Payments and Finance owners. Lock live authority/source/draft, compare accepted commercial conditions, atomically enforce limited promotion capacity and allocate stock only at committed placement through existing semantic operations. Retry a complete serialization/deadlock transaction with fresh evaluation. Never turn the quotation snapshot into a price/stock/promotion hold or consume an acknowledged quote through legacy Orders without the missing contract.
6. Payment obligations/receipts/claims remain distinct: actual funds require independent trusted evidence, idempotency and reconciliation. Manual shopper claims are untrusted; partial/over/duplicate/unknown outcomes, bounded review aging, stock-release/refund liability and Admin review need existing Payments/Finance semantics. Provider work/outbox processing stays outside authoritative commit; delivery failure cannot rewrite business truth.
