## Stock recovery correction

The Bag offered a one-unit decrement for stock-short lines. If a line of three dropped to one available unit, the first decrement requested two and failed server validation. The shopper had no quantity control that could reach the valid value. The projection now includes an optional `suggestedQuantity` for positive stock shortfalls and incorporates it in the calculation fingerprint. The Bag offers an explicit reduction to that quantity through the existing Cart controller and quantity command. The suggestion is advisory and the command rechecks current availability. Zero-stock, hidden and invalid lines continue through replacement or removal. The database regression covers rejection of two, acceptance of one, stable line identity and one revision increment.

## Manual acceptance procedure

1. Use a fresh synthetic tenant and isolated profile for each engine and device. Run current Safari and Firefox on desktop and Safari on an iPhone and iPad plus Chrome on an Android phone. Run VoiceOver on macOS and iOS and a supported desktop screen reader with Firefox. Record OS, engine, assistive technology, device, orientation, viewport and zoom.
2. From a product listing, open the configurable PDP. Using keyboard only, choose each required option and add two different variants. Check the Bag count, open the drawer, cycle focus in both directions and close with Escape. Confirm focus returns to the actual trigger. Open the full Bag and verify the route heading, accessible control names and feedback announcements.
3. Change the first line to the second variant. Confirm that the collision preview announces the combined quantity, the target line survives, and the API returns the same selection and quantity as the UI. At native 200% and 400% zoom verify visible focus, no horizontal page overflow, reachable controls and a usable Clear confirmation. On touch hardware repeat in portrait and landscape and apply a coupon with the keyboard open.
4. With the Bag open, reduce available stock below the selected quantity, reprice, expire the coupon and invalidate the configuration in separate fixture steps. Refresh through focus or reconnect. Check retained intent, updated totals, suggested reduction or removal, and live feedback. Confirm changes against current Cart API responses, recording identifiers and revisions without credentials.
5. On a throttled connection, interrupt before send and separately after commit. Restore connectivity, verify the uncertain-action message and replay the same key and body. Check the revision and surviving line once. Race edits from two tabs and verify stale intent prompts review without overwriting. Block cookies or expire the session and verify no automatic Cart creation, guest substitution or disclosure. Use explicit recovery and check a second tab cannot resurrect the old authority. Cancel and then confirm Clear with items and coupons selected.

## Policy and release

Product-specific minima, maxima and increments, location/channel/destination eligibility and backorder handling require owner decisions. Compare-at and tax presentation, promotion stacking/exclusions, currency semantics and redemption enforcement also remain open. Cart reports current merchandise amounts and does not establish delivery eligibility, reservations or final Checkout amounts.

Persistent upgrade continuity and legacy credential cutover, backup/restore and revocation drills, deployed HTTPS proxy/private-cache and database role checks, retention and representative concurrent load remain release gates. Fresh and synthetic populated upgrades provide local evidence only. The account boundary and merge design are recorded in the [Phase 03A backend handoff](PHASE_03A_ACCOUNT_CART_HANDOFF.md).
