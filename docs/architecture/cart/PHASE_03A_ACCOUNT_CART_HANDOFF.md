# Phase 03A Account Cart backend handoff

**Design handoff. Account Cart implementation and acceptance remain open.**

## Authority and lifecycle

- Reuse the strict resolver returned by `registerShopperAuthRoutes`. Derive tenant, account and customer from the verified `ShopperPrincipal` and the configured Storefront context. The existing Cart request resolver already recognizes this authority and fences failed authentication. Preserve rejection on invalid sessions, resolver failures and tenant mismatches. Keep Checkout's current refusal of account authority until its own integration.
- Migration 3213 already supplies the tenant-scoped shopper-account foreign key and the partial unique index for one ACTIVE Cart per tenant, account and currency. Verify the owner, customer and credential-state constraints against actual inserts before extending the schema. Account Carts require no guest capability. Separate persistent account Cart lifecycle from guest credential TTL. Resolve concurrent creation through the database uniqueness rule and deterministic owner-level coordination. Keep GET free of creation side effects.
- Extract the shared Cart projection and mutation core at the authorized aggregate boundary. Today `findCart`, the projection entry point and the receipt wrapper are coupled to guest tokens. Keep credential resolution distinct from operations on an already authorized, tenant-scoped Cart. Preserve the aggregate lock, owner-context check, revision, exact-key receipt, bounded repeatable-read retries and outbox. Keep account and guest capability checks inside their respective transactional paths.
- On sign-in, sign-out or session loss, fence the browser queue and discard stale owner responses. Resolve known and uncertain guest intent before merge. Preserve any unresolved intent under its original owner with an explicit recovery path. A failed account session requires reauthentication. Any return to a guest Bag requires resolving the guest context deliberately. Invalidation messages should carry partition and revision only. Across devices, refetch on focus or explicit refresh and let a competing write return a revision conflict. Reconcile each response against the active owner generation.

## Merge review and commit

1. Preview under verified account authority and separately prove the exact guest slot and selected context. Bind the preview to both owner contexts, both intent revisions and the material commerce evaluation. Multiple guest slots require explicit selection. Keep preview read-only and redact unavailable or unpublished details.
2. Produce a deterministic plan. Retain the account line ID for identical variants, keep distinct variants separate and define stable ordering for guest-only additions. Surface over-cap allocations and invalid lines for explicit review. Reuse Catalog, Pricing, Inventory and Promotions evaluation with an explicitly approved account preview policy. Retain account coupons and require explicit action for guest candidates. Decide how reduced stock, invalid configurations and any excluded quantities appear before committing the merge.
3. Commit under an account-bound exact intent key. Establish one lock order for account creation, merge, ordinary edits and cleanup, with Cart rows locked in deterministic ID order. Revalidate both revisions and the material commerce outcome inside a coherent transaction. A stock, price or coupon change that alters the plan requires renewed review even when neither Cart intent revision changed. Avoid treating evaluation time alone as material change.
4. Atomically persist account lines, the resulting revision, guest MERGED status and capability revocation, durable account-bound receipt and outbox. Define the revision and event behavior for a merge with no account-line changes. Check for an authorized committed receipt before requiring the now-revoked guest credential on exact replay. Validate the original intent digest and both owner bindings. Return the immutable merge outcome separately from the fresh account projection.
5. Prove the implementation with real PostgreSQL and API cases for concurrent account creation, account edit versus merge, parallel merges of one guest, lost response after commit, stale review at unchanged revisions, rollback after partial writes, revocation, expired session, wrong account and wrong tenant. Verify the previous guest routes, replay behavior and Checkout refusal remain intact.

Resolve account Cart retention, empty merge behavior, account promotion preview and over-cap or unavailable-item handling during 03A design. Inventory remains advisory and a merge establishes neither a reservation nor a final Checkout amount.
