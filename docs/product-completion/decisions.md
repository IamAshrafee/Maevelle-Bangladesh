# Product Completion Decisions

## 2026-08-29 — Complete platform supersedes MVP and scoped plans

Maevelle is no longer targeting an MVP, V1 slice, or old phased implementation
boundary. Historical "future," "deferred," and roadmap labels are retained only
as research context. Current work should build complete, production-quality
modules across backend and frontend, while the solo heavy-development policy
keeps migrations, verification, and documentation effort proportional.

Work may occur in any user-selected area. The current-focus tracker provides a
resume point, not an exclusive queue.

## 2026-08-26 — Continuous product completion supersedes launch sequencing

Historical roadmap and release documents remain evidence and operational
reference. They no longer control product-development priority. The tracker and
current repository evidence choose one active area at a time.

## 2026-08-26 — Catalog Product Management is the first active area

Catalog was selected for business leverage, visible owner usability impact,
clear domain ownership, and its role as the identity dependency for most
commerce and operations. It remains the recommended resume area until verified,
genuinely blocked, or superseded by a newer user-selected priority.

## 2026-08-26 — Readiness must be server-derived

Admin must never imply that price, media, stock, attributes, or variant setup is
ready from static UI. Publication blockers must use the same authoritative
validation as the publish command. Non-blocking operational signals must be
visually and contractually distinct from blockers.
