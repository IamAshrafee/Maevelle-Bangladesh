# Catalog Product Management Plan

1. Product worklist, server pagination/filtering, and truthful readiness.
2. Product overview editing, deep links, stale-save recovery, and unsaved changes.
3. Taxonomy, attributes, structured information, FAQ, and SEO.
4. Variant matrix, lifecycle, identifiers, and cross-domain price/stock/media.
5. Duplicate, archive/reactivate, and safe bulk operations.
6. Activity/audit, cleanup, accessibility, responsive and performance hardening.
7. Realistic end-to-end operator and Storefront verification.

Only this parent area is active. Cross-domain changes are allowed when needed to
complete these stages.
