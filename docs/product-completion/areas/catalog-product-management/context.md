# Catalog Product Management Context

## Business purpose

Give Maevelle operators one coherent place to create, organize, validate,
publish, maintain, and retire Products and their sellable Variants without
needing database knowledge or bouncing blindly among disconnected consoles.

## Users and jobs

- Owner/catalog manager: create and merchandise Products safely.
- Operations: identify Products blocked from sale or needing setup.
- Pricing/media/inventory users: reach the correct Variant context quickly.
- Customer-facing teams: trust that published Products have valid public data.

## Domain boundary

Catalog owns identity, lifecycle, taxonomy, options, variants, structured
attributes, information groups, FAQ, and publication intent. Pricing, inventory,
stored media, sizing, procurement, reviews, and analytics remain separate truth.
