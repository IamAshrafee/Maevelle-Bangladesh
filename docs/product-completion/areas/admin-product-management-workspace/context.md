# Admin Product Management Workspace

This area is the operator workflow for creating, organizing, merchandising,
publishing and maintaining products. It composes Catalog-owned identity,
taxonomy, variants and publication with read/write paths owned by pricing,
media, sizing and inventory.

Current evidence: dedicated Product list/create/detail/edit routes and focused
components exist after recent rebuild commits. No V2 assessment has yet proven
the complete operator workflow or current Storefront result.
