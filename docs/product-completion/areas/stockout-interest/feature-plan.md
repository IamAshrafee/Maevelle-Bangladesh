# Omnichannel stockout interest and restock alerts: implementation plan

**Status:** design and source assessment, 2026-10-30. No feature code has been implemented.\
**Product owner:** Catalog/Inventory for availability; Customer Engagement for interest and contact; Notifications for delivery.\
**Goal:** let a shopper express demand for a specific unavailable item in seconds, optionally request one restock alert, and give staff a reliable, privacy-safe view of demand across repeated stockout periods.

## 2. Product decisions

1. **Copy and intent.** Use **“Notify me when available”** as the entry action on a sold-out selected variant, then present clear ways to express interest: send a message, request a one-time email/SMS alert, or show interest without contact. A message route promises only that Maevelle receives an inquiry when it is actually sent and matched. An alert route promises one notification only after contact verification, purpose-specific permission, and channel eligibility. Its pending state says “Check your message to confirm your alert”; its confirmed state says “You're on the list for one restock alert.” Anonymous interest contributes an aggregate demand signal only. Avoid “preorder,” “reserve,” “join queue,” or a promised restock date.
2. **Variant first.** Require a complete option selection before submission. Show unavailable options with a clear label, while allowing selection so shoppers can request the exact color/size. If every variant is out, select a reasonable variant only when the product has exactly one; otherwise prompt for the variant. If another variant is available, keep its normal purchase actions.
3. **Short flow.** A single adaptive sheet/dialog asks for one contact method: mobile number (labeled “SMS or call number,” with “WhatsApp on this number” as an optional preference, not a delivery promise) or email. Use a single required contact value for alerts, optional first name, and an unchecked purpose-specific alert checkbox with plain privacy wording. Anonymous interest needs no contact field. No account creation, address, or full CRM form in this flow. Submission creates a pending alert; one tap on a short-lived link sent to that channel verifies contact control. Do not require the shopper to remain on the PDP or enter an OTP in the initial form. The confirmation must say “Check your message to confirm your alert,” and the verification message must include a cancellation path. Authenticated customers can also see their requests in account context if that surface exists at implementation time.
4. **Interest is not a hold.** No inventory reservation, purchase right, priority allocation, price lock, or guarantee. If stock returns before form submission, explain this immediately and show Add to Bag for the selected variant; optionally record the attempted interest as analytics only with consent, never as active unmet demand.
5. **Demand and notification are distinct.** A valid interest remains historical even after restock, alert delivery, cancellation, product archival, or deletion of personal contact. The alert subscription is one-shot per stockout episode. Staff can see counts without gaining access to contact data.
6. **Messaging option.** The sheet also offers “Message us about this item.” Website chat is available now and receives the PDP variant context. Offer a WhatsApp prefilled-message handoff only when Maevelle has an actively maintained business number and clear operator ownership; its message is a customer-initiated conversation, not an automatically recorded interest or an alert subscription. A “Copy request” action can support Messenger, Instagram or any app. A matched inbound inquiry gets “We received your interest”; it never gets “You're on the alert list” without a separately confirmed alert subscription.

## 3. Domain model and lifecycle

### Stockout episodes

Define an episode per `(organization_id, variant_id)`, opened when an eligible, published, priced, active variant's **authoritative sellable ATS reaches zero** and closed when that variant becomes purchasable again. Record `opened_at`, `closed_at`, `close_reason`, and availability observation/source references. The exact condition must share the sellability resolver used by storefront/cart/checkout; do not equate a cached search projection, on-hand total, missing price, or catalog disablement with a stockout.

Inventory changes currently emit committed outbox events, but their payloads often identify only the changed aggregate. The Storefront search consumer also uses an event allowlist that omits `inventory.reservation.expired`. Re-reading only the current balance after an asynchronous event can collapse a fast zero → positive → zero sequence into one observed state. Therefore, add an **authoritative per-variant sellability change record** in the same transaction as every operation that can change ATS or eligibility: inventory adjustment/condition move, reservation create/release/expiry, transfer, receiving, fulfillment, return, stocktake, and relevant catalog/pricing/publication changes. Under a shared per-variant lock, capture before/after eligible sellable quantity and eligibility reason, committed event ID, monotonic variant sequence, and occurrence time whenever either value changes. The episode projector derives zero/positive boundaries and later positive-stock increases from this ordered record idempotently; a periodic bounded reconciliation detects missing sequences and current-state drift, repairs the latter with an explicit `RECONCILED` cause, and bootstraps already-out variants on rollout. It must not invent unrecorded historical transitions. The public submit path can materialize the current episode transactionally if projection is lagging, using the same lock and transition sequence. A unique partial index permits at most one open episode. A rapid zero → positive → zero sequence creates two episodes; a duplicate event does not. Record `observed_at` separately from committed transition time.

When stock becomes positive, close the episode and create an idempotent `restock.detected` outbox fact from the ordered transition; sending occurs asynchronously. If stock disappears again before dispatch, cancel the pending wave and close unsent subscriptions from the prior episode as `EXPIRED_NEW_STOCKOUT`, with an optional fresh opt-in path; do not silently carry their permission into the next episode. Define a short stabilization window (proposed 10 minutes, configurable) and recheck ATS, publication, price, suppression, and subscription at dispatch. The window protects against temporary reconciliation/reversal.

**Small-restock policy:** automatic first-wave sends are limited to `min(current ATS whole units, configured maximum recipients per wave)` and ordered by verified subscription time, then request ID. The cap limits messages, not inventory; it grants no claim to units. The remaining verified requests stay `ACTIVE_WAITING` for later positive stock increases **while the product remains available after this episode**. A later increase can start another bounded wave after stabilization. If stock returns to zero, expire these unsent requests with an explanation in Admin, since a new stockout episode has begun. Staff approval is required to override a configured cap or retry a paused wave; there is no automatic blast to all 25 shoppers when 3 units arrive. Recheck stock before every batch and stop if it depletes. Communicate clearly that alert recipients may still find the item sold out.

Use composite organization-scoped foreign keys, explicit delete behavior, `timestamptz`, restrained indexes matching the work queue and product-detail queries, and no broad PII index. A keyed HMAC of normalized contact plus organization and purpose supports dedupe without exposing the value. Rotation and lookup policy need a documented key-management path. Data-subject export and erasure must include these records; anonymization removes contact and dedupe material while leaving aggregate event counts only where policy permits. Set retention from a counsel-approved schedule before launch, with a cleanup job and audit evidence.

### State semantics

Alert subscription states are `PENDING_VERIFICATION`, `ACTIVE`, `ACTIVE_WAITING`, `NOTIFIED`, `CANCELLED`, and `EXPIRED`, with reason and timestamp. Only `ACTIVE` or `ACTIVE_WAITING` with verified contact and purpose-specific permission can be dispatched. Verification expires after a bounded period (proposed 48 hours); pending requests still count in total requests but never in **contactable demand**. Verification is a one-time link or equivalent channel proof, not a Customer 360 identity link. The verification message identifies the product/variant, provides confirm and cancel paths, and contains no marketing. A signed browser receipt can cancel while still in the original session; after leaving, the channel message provides the durable cancellation path. Links are scoped, single-use where appropriate, time-limited, stored as hashes, and excluded from referrers/analytics; a verified subscriber can request a fresh manage link through a rate-limited neutral response. A verified account contact may use existing trusted account assurance only if the applicable identity challenge has actually been completed; otherwise it follows the same verification path.

`NOTIFIED`, `CANCELLED`, `EXPIRED`, and `ANONYMIZED` remain period history but not active contactable demand. An anonymous signal is counted once per reasonable browser token/episode and cannot become contactable until the shopper explicitly supplies contact and accepts the notice. Do not claim “unique customers” from anonymous tokens or unverified numbers. Show **total requests**, **verified contactable requests**, and **quarantined/suspected duplicates** separately. Verified customer dedupe can use canonical Customer ID only after trusted linking; unauthenticated contact dedupe uses keyed normalized contact. Repeat submissions return a neutral result without automatically changing the original permission or contact; changes require control of that contact. A new episode permits a new request; old consent does not silently subscribe to it.

### Evidence quality and identity boundaries

| Observation | Demand treatment | Identity/contact conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Sheet open, channel link open, copied text | Measurement only when analytics consent allows; zero operational requests. | None. |
| Anonymous button accepted | Low-confidence demand signal, deduped by bounded browser token and episode, with abuse controls. | No contact or verified person. |
| Contact form submitted, confirmation pending | Pending request shown separately from alert-eligible demand. | Address/number control unproved. |
| Matched Website, Facebook or Instagram inbound message | Provider- or session-confirmed inquiry after content/reference validation, dedupe and abuse checks. | Control of that conversation only; no cross-channel Customer identity and no future-alert permission. |
| Staff verifies a real unmatched inquiry | Manually attributed demand with actor, reason and conversation evidence; separately reported. | No automatic identity or alert permission. |
| Contact confirmed and purpose accepted | Alert-eligible request only while delivery policy permits the chosen channel. | Channel contact control for this purpose, not a purchase prediction. |

Deduplicate repeated inbound messages by `(organization, episode, variant, provider channel, provider sender identity)` and provider message ID; ten messages from one account about the same variant normally count as one interest. A valid reference forwarded to another account can create a separate channel inquiry only after that account sends its own message; the reference never merges accounts or proves a common person. Apply abuse signals for bursts across variants, repeated reference creation, suspicious provider accounts and anomalous traffic. Quarantine suspect requests for staff review without silently discarding real inquiries. Human-confirmed means an operator reviewed the inquiry, not that the person or purchasing intent is certain. Admin must never label any tier as “guaranteed buyers.”

### Messaging handoff and inbound proof

One shared Storefront sheet chooses a channel adapter based on actual connection and provider capability. A shopper may always stay on the website or use the anonymous option; external messaging is never mandatory.

| Route | Prepared message | Automatic interest confirmation today | Restock alert eligibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website chat | Product/variant context is attached by the existing PDP chat integration. | Extend canonical inbound chat handling to register an explicit interest message. | Use a separately confirmed email/SMS contact unless a durable website-chat notification route is proven. |
| WhatsApp | `wa.me` can prefill text to Maevelle's configured number; shopper presses Send. | None until WhatsApp joins the unified inbox; operator may record an actually received inquiry as manual demand. | No automatic WhatsApp promise until provider integration, opt-in and template/window eligibility are implemented. |
| Messenger | Open Maevelle's connected Page chat; offer Copy request. | Extend the existing inbound webhook matcher when the message/reference arrives. | Only under current provider re-engagement policy; otherwise offer email/SMS. |
| Instagram | Open Maevelle's connected professional account DM where supported; offer Copy request. | Extend the existing inbound webhook matcher when the message/reference arrives. | Only under current provider re-engagement policy; otherwise offer email/SMS. |

Use a short, human-readable message such as “Hi Maevelle, I’m interested in [product title] — [selected option labels]. Please let me know if it returns. Ref: [short request code].” The code is random, short-lived, scoped to organization/product/variant/episode, and contains no customer data or hidden authorization. Limit message length and use a canonical product link without tracking or personal parameters. WhatsApp's official click-to-chat format can prefill this text in a chat with the configured business number; the shopper must still press Send. [WhatsApp Help Center: click to chat](https://faq.whatsapp.com/5913398998672934/).

The handoff has three distinct observations: `PREPARED` (sheet created a reference), `OPENED_OR_COPIED` (browser best-effort only), and `INBOUND_CONFIRMED` (trusted provider webhook or Website-chat message actually arrived). Only an inbound message that **explicitly expresses interest** creates/links a provider-confirmed demand request, after server validation of the reference, sender channel, destination business account, variant, episode and provider message ID. A valid copied reference can arrive through any connected business channel; the optional handoff target is for UX/attribution, not authority. Consume or bind the reference atomically and dedupe repeat webhooks. If the text was edited or the code omitted, staff can attach an actually received conversation manually with an audited action and an explicit `MANUALLY_ATTRIBUTED` quality label; do not guess from a product title alone. A copied code is a correlation hint, not authentication: possession alone cannot verify an email, phone, Customer profile, or permission to send an alert. Rate-limit reference creation and inbound matching, expire unused references, and avoid logging full message bodies in the interest domain.

Website chat already stores server-validated product/variant context and therefore can link an explicit customer-initiated interest message without a copied code, but its visitor session does not prove ownership of an external phone or email. Facebook/Instagram inbound identity can establish control of that provider conversation; it does not authorize future restock messages outside provider rules. Today, WhatsApp is **not** an inbox channel, so a WhatsApp click-to-chat message cannot be automatically confirmed or automatically notified through this feature. Until an official WhatsApp inbound/outbound adapter exists, show the WhatsApp route as a manual-contact path only, or hide it if operators cannot reliably handle replies. Do not count its clicks in verified or contactable demand. An operator may record a received WhatsApp message as manually attributed demand, clearly separate from provider-confirmed counts, but cannot turn it into an automated WhatsApp alert without channel proof and permission. A future WhatsApp adapter must join the canonical inbox and enforce verified inbound identity, explicit restock permission, approved template/window rules, suppression, delivery status, and the same one-shot dispatch controls before WhatsApp alerts are offered.

For Messenger/Instagram, use only provider-supported customer-initiated links and connection-healthy destinations; a copied message is the portable fallback because a WhatsApp-style text prefill must not be assumed on those platforms. Their existing inbound webhook pipeline can be extended to recognize the reference and attach the canonical conversation. Provider referral metadata, if supported and present, is a supplementary source hint rather than a substitute for a received message and server-validated product reference. Restock alerts over those channels require separate policy and provider eligibility checks at send time; where those checks cannot be met, offer email/SMS confirmation instead. The outbound recipient is always the verified inbound channel identity, never a name typed into the message. Keep the intent and permission for one restock alert distinct from ordinary conversation replies and marketing. A conversation may later support an in-stock product inquiry or assisted order through existing Messaging/Commerce operations; this stockout request must not mutate those workflows or infer an order.

## 4. API and application boundaries

Define additive DTOs in `packages/contracts`, with typed success/error envelopes and bounded pagination. Suggested routes:

| Route | Behavior |
|---|---|
| `GET /storefront/v1/products/:handle/stockout-interest?variantId=...` | Public eligibility and current request state only if a safe opaque token exists; no PII in public response. May be folded into product detail if that avoids an extra request. |
| `POST /storefront/v1/stockout-interests` | Validate organization/product/variant and current stock atomically; accept mode, contact channel/value, notice acceptance, stable client idempotency key; return `created`, `alreadyRecorded`, or typed `NOW_AVAILABLE`, `NOT_ELIGIBLE`, `RATE_LIMITED`. Return the same generic result for contact duplicates to avoid enumeration. |
| `DELETE /storefront/v1/stockout-interests/:token` | Signed, scoped cancellation, idempotent; alternate email/SMS manage link must verify control before exposing detail. |
| `POST /storefront/v1/stockout-interests/:token/verification` | Single-use channel proof activates the subscription; expired/invalid links return a generic recoverable state without leaking whether a contact exists. |
| `POST /storefront/v1/stockout-interest-handoffs` | Create a scoped short-lived reference and return safe prefilled text plus enabled channel destinations; does not create a counted interest. |
| `GET /admin/stockout-interest` | Capability-gated cursor-paged product/variant demand queue; filters for open episode, contactable count, age, category, supplier/incoming stock, and status. |
| `GET /admin/products/:id/stockout-interest` | Episode timeline, current counts, trends, and paged request detail only under sensitive-contact capability. |
| `POST /admin/stockout-interest/:id/actions` | Audited bounded actions: suppress/cancel alert, mark contact outcome, resend only under explicit policy and one-shot safeguards. No manual stock mutation. |

The engagement service owns request persistence, dedupe and episode state. Catalog and Inventory own sellability, Procurement owns incoming supply, Notifications owns delivery, Customers owns identity/consent, Analytics owns measurement. The API orchestrates those services; it does not calculate stock itself. Use the existing auth/capability context and organization isolation; public writes derive organization from the storefront context and re-resolve the product, not from a caller-trusted organization ID. Add rate limits by IP and keyed contact fingerprint, bot trap, bounded body size and abuse monitoring; use a neutral response to avoid phone/email harvesting. Validate Bangladesh phone format plus international E.164, and email normalization without overaggressive canonicalization. CSRF and cookie behavior must follow the existing storefront mutation pattern.

Existing Notifications customer deliveries require a Customer recipient. Design a **purpose-scoped guest recipient** extension to its intent/recipient contract or an equally narrow adapter into its existing email/SMS transport, suppression, retry, idempotency, audit, and webhook machinery. Before verification, this recipient is eligible only for the bounded confirmation/cancellation message; after verification and consent checks, it can receive the one restock alert. Do not manufacture a Customer profile or infer a Customer identity merely to send these messages. Keep confirmation and restock purposes distinct and record the source interest ID. This is a contract decision to resolve before migration design; a separate provider-sending engine is not acceptable.

## 5. Storefront experience

- **PDP placement:** put the action in the commercial decision area in desktop, tablet, and mobile; synchronize the mobile inline action, sticky bar and desktop purchase block from one shared availability/action component. Hide quantity controls for an unavailable selection. State “Currently unavailable” and never imply it can be purchased. Keep option selection and relevant cross-sells visible.
- **Sheet content:** title with selected product and option labels, one contact method switch, one field, concise purpose notice, one primary submit button, anonymous alternative, and a no-pressure dismissal. Use phone keyboard/input mode and `autocomplete`, field help, accessible labels and error text. The sheet must scroll above the mobile keyboard, support escape/back behavior, and restore focus.
- **Messaging choice:** show Website chat and enabled external handoffs as alternate actions in the same sheet, with one-tap WhatsApp opening and a separate “Copy request” button. Explain “Send the prepared message to tell us you're interested”; after opening an external app, show a neutral “Return here anytime” state, never a false success. Keep the direct email/SMS request available for customers who want an automated alert without a chat. Do not require a customer to copy text when using the WhatsApp prefilled link.
- **Success and recovery:** inline acknowledgement with selected variant, chosen channel, expected one-time nature, manage/cancel path, and “Explore similar items” link. For `NOW_AVAILABLE`, close the sheet and offer purchase with fresh availability. For temporary failure, preserve typed input and allow retry with the same idempotency key. For an invalid or unreachable contact, give a correction path. Do not show a fake success if persistence failed.
- **Optional authenticated path:** prefill a verified contact only with clear confirmation; never auto-subscribe from account presence, wishlist membership, cart history, or CRM association. Keep guest flow first class.
- **Other surfaces:** product cards can show “Out of stock” and link to the PDP; do not add an interest form to every card or create crawler-dependent modals. An Admin-configurable feature switch and per-product exclusion may be needed for genuinely discontinued items; if excluded, show alternatives rather than an alert promise.

## 6. Admin information architecture and operations

Create **Stockout interest** as a worklist reachable from Inventory replenishment and Product management, with its own route because cross-product demand and contact follow-up need filtering and paging. Add a compact summary card to the Product detail page and a “Demand” column/sort signal in replenishment. Use the existing Admin page-shell, Table, StatusBadge, responsive filter and detail primitives; mobile uses cards or horizontal scroll for the dense table, with reachable actions and safe PII reveal. Do not make the Analytics dashboard the operational queue.

The list shows product/variant, current episode start and age, total requests, provider-confirmed message interest, manually attributed message interest, verified **alert-eligible** requests, quarantined/suspected duplicates, 7/30-day request trend, current ATS, incoming purchase/transfer quantity, last restock, and state (`OPEN`, `RESTOCKED_PENDING`, `NOTIFYING`, `CLOSED`, `NEEDS_ATTENTION`). Sort by a transparent demand score (e.g. recent verified requests and age), but always display raw counts and explain that requests are not forecast sales units. Product detail shows every episode with start/end, stock transition evidence, requests by time, sent/delivered/suppressed results, and a paged contact list. If legitimate attribution permits, show **observed post-restock purchases by interested customers** separately from requests; do not imply every purchase was caused by the alert. Contact fields are masked by default and require a separate `stockout_interest.contacts.view` capability; export requires its own capability and audit. General `stockout_interest.view`, `manage`, and `settings.manage` capabilities do not imply sensitive access.

Operational actions: inspect demand, jump to replenishment policy or Purchase/Transfer handoff, review paused dispatches, suppress an individual alert, record customer contact, and inspect delivery failure. Staff cannot mark “restocked” manually; they must post authoritative stock through existing flows. Notifications Center shows delivery attempts and failure reasons. Provide a setting for channels, stabilization delay, send cap, retention, and product exclusions, with validated defaults and change audit. Start with SMS/email where supported. WhatsApp sending requires a separately approved provider integration and channel-specific policy; a WhatsApp-capable number may still receive SMS only when the shopper explicitly chose SMS.

## 7. SEO, crawlers and external measurement

**Search appearance.** Keep an active, published product's canonical URL returning 200 while temporarily sold out. Keep its title, description, images, FAQs, internal links, and Product JSON-LD; represent each priced offer's real `InStock`/`OutOfStock` state from the same authoritative availability snapshot as the visible PDP. Do not turn a temporary stockout into a 404, `noindex`, canonical to category, or a new query-string variant URL. Permanently discontinued/unpublished products follow a separate lifecycle decision (helpful alternatives, redirect only for true replacement, or 404/410 when appropriate). Google explicitly recommends leaving temporarily unavailable product pages accessible and marking them out of stock, and documents `OutOfStock` in Product structured data. [Google Search Central: site pause and stock status](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/pause-online-business), [Product structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product-snippet).

**Freshness.** Reconcile Product JSON-LD, visible button, API, search projection and product feeds after inventory commit. Public PDP currently fetches without persistent cache, but browser state can be stale; refresh availability on form submit and before purchase. Add paged published-product URLs to sitemap and test canonical redirects/handle changes. Do not put personal tokens, form success states, or episode IDs in sitemap or analytics page locations. Run Rich Results Test and URL Inspection on representative stocked, fully sold-out, partially sold-out, and discontinued pages. Rich results are not guaranteed.

**Event taxonomy.** Add versioned vendor-neutral events: `STOCKOUT_INTEREST_OPENED`, `STOCKOUT_HANDOFF_OPENED`, `STOCKOUT_MESSAGE_MATCHED`, `STOCKOUT_INTEREST_SUBMITTED`, `STOCKOUT_INTEREST_ALREADY_RECORDED`, `STOCKOUT_INTEREST_CANCELLED`, and server-side `RESTOCK_ALERT_QUEUED`/`RESTOCK_ALERT_DELIVERED` where their semantics are useful. Include product/variant/SKU, episode ID only as an internal opaque dimension, availability at action, UI placement, mode (`ANONYMOUS_SIGNAL`, `MESSAGE_INTEREST` or `RESTOCK_ALERT`), channel class, evidence quality, and outcome. Exclude phone, email, name, free text, signed links, raw URLs, Customer ID, and contact fingerprints. The server's accepted request or matched inbound message is the source of truth for Admin counts; browser event loss must not change them. Use stable event IDs to dedupe retry/client-server dual delivery.

**GA4 and ads.** The repo has a vendor-neutral event bus and commerce item mapping, but this source review did not establish a live GA4 destination adapter. Implement or extend that central adapter as needed, mapping accepted alert requests to a separately named custom event such as `restock_alert_requested`; optionally map to `generate_lead` only if the business explicitly treats this as a lead and reports it consistently. Anonymous signals must not be leads. Register only useful non-PII dimensions, mark the event as a key event only after measurement review, and compare GA4 to server counts with expected consent/ad-block gaps. Google's recommended `generate_lead` is for initial lead acquisition, while this product request is a narrower business fact. [GA4 recommended events](https://developers.google.com/analytics/devguides/collection/ga4/reference/events). Send to Google/Meta only under the existing consent model; add no direct vendor calls in the form handler. If Google Consent Mode is used, keep consent defaults and updates in the central adapter, including `analytics_storage`, `ad_storage`, `ad_user_data`, and `ad_personalization` as applicable. [Google consent mode guide](https://developers.google.com/tag-platform/security/guides/consent). Do not transmit submitted contact data or use enhanced conversions without a separate lawful-purpose and consent review.

**Reports.** Measure PDP stockout views → form opens → accepted contactable requests → alerts queued/delivered → subsequent visits/orders where attribution is legitimately available. Report anonymous signal and verified contact rates separately; avoid presenting alert delivery as a sale or causal lift. Dashboard aggregate counts must not expose low-volume contact identities.

## 8. Scenario and failure matrix

| Scenario | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| One size sold out, others available | Interest action for selected sold-out size only; other sizes retain purchase. |
| Product entirely sold out | Keep PDP indexable; show selection and one clear interest action; show alternatives. |
| Zero stock caused by reservation, then reservation expires | Episode opens/closes against sellable ATS; stabilization and recheck prevent premature alert. |
| Stock arrives between page load and submit | API returns `NOW_AVAILABLE`; shopper sees refreshed purchase action; no active demand record. |
| Two simultaneous submissions or retry after timeout | Atomic idempotency and dedupe yield one request; same key with changed body is rejected. |
| One shopper requests multiple variants | Separate variant requests; one per variant/episode; no accidental cross-variant alert. |
| Repeated stockout after restock | New episode and new opt-in opportunity; historical periods and outcomes remain visible. |
| Product unpublished, archived, unpriced, or variant deleted | No new interest; pending alerts cancel with reason; retain allowed aggregate history and resolve FK/deletion policy. |
| Tiny restock or quick reversal | Queue is held/capped; worker rechecks sellable stock and publication before each batch. |
| Contact is submitted but not verified | Show pending state and a resend path with throttling; count as total request only; never send restock alert. |
| First restock has fewer units than verified requests | Oldest verified requests receive one bounded wave; remaining requests wait only while that episode's restock remains positive. A new stockout expires them and requires fresh opt-in. |
| Bad/duplicate/changed contact | Validate and normalize; neutral duplicate response; manage/verification flow for corrections; no customer merge from contact equality alone. |
| Customer withdraws consent, suppresses channel, requests erasure | Cancel eligible dispatch, honor suppression at final send check, remove contact on schedule/request, keep only permitted aggregate evidence. |
| SMS/email provider failure or unknown outcome | Use existing notification retry/reconciliation; avoid blind resend; show actionable Admin state and accurate sent/delivered counts. |
| Bot/fraudulent interest spike | Throttle and quarantine suspect signals; expose quality flags, not inflated “unique customers”; no direct public contact lookup. |
| Analytics denied or blocked | Operational request still works; no optional analytics event to vendors. |
| WhatsApp opened but message not sent; copied text never pasted | Handoff expires; no verified request or alert subscription is counted. |
| Message arrives with a valid reference through a connected provider | Atomically bind the provider message/conversation to one variant request; use the sender's provider identity, not the copied code, as channel proof. |
| Same account sends the message repeatedly or forwards its code | Count one request per account/variant/episode; a different account is separate and never silently linked to the first. |
| Inbound inquiry is confirmed but restock is weeks away | Preserve demand; offer a separately verified email/SMS alert if provider re-engagement is unavailable, and do not promise a Messenger/Instagram/WhatsApp alert. |
| Reference is shared, stale, edited out, or arrives after stock returns | Do not auto-verify an unrelated sender or create active unmet demand; show a safe manual review path with attribution quality. |
| Staff lacks PII permission or belongs to another organization | Aggregate only or 403; no cross-tenant inference through counts, IDs, exports, logs or notification actions. |

## 9. Implementation sequence and completion gates

1. **Contract and invariant design:** name sellability reasons and stockout eligibility; specify DTOs, verification and subscription state transitions, guest notification recipient extension, messaging handoff/inbound-proof contract, small-restock wave policy, purpose-specific notice, retention and delivery channel policy; decide exact Admin navigation with the current shared layout. Resolve legal/privacy wording before public contact collection.
2. **Migration and domain:** add schema, constraints, indexes, capability seeds and forward migration; cover every authoritative sellability mutation with ordered transition recording, episode projection/reconciliation, request dedupe, immutable events, deletion/anonymization and outbox. Test fresh and prior-schema upgrade in the focused migration suite.
3. **API:** add public and Admin endpoints with boundary validation, idempotency, authorization, rate limiting, pagination, masked PII, audited actions, and stable errors. Test concurrency and tenant isolation.
4. **Storefront:** repair the Buy Now failure navigation as an independently verifiable fix; build one shared PDP availability/action behavior across mobile/sticky/desktop, responsive sheet, success/error/retry/manage paths. Test variant selection, keyboard/touch, screen reader labels, and stock changes while open.
5. **Admin:** build demand queue, Product episode section, replenishment handoff, notification outcomes, settings and capability-controlled contact views. Verify real operator workflow on mobile, tablet and desktop.
6. **Delivery and measurement:** wire verification and one-shot restock dispatch into existing Notifications with final eligibility/consent/suppression checks; add event bus and consent-gated GA4 adapter mapping; align structured data, sitemap, search and feeds; observe worker failures and reconciliation lag.
7. **Acceptance:** focused DB/API/UI tests for the scenario matrix, migration upgrade, SEO markup, accessibility/responsive visual review, synthetic end-to-end stockout → interest → restock → one alert → repeat stockout, privacy export/erasure and provider failure. Then owner-led UI/operational review and a controlled staging deployment with release SHA/digests, migration and smoke evidence. Do not call the feature complete if an implemented channel or contact-management path is still missing.

These steps may be merged as reviewable implementation checkpoints, with a feature switch keeping incomplete public flows unavailable. A checkpoint is not a completed feature. The stockout-to-confirmation-to-restock-alert customer path, its Admin control surface, privacy/tenant controls, and SEO/measurement behavior in this plan form the completion gate. Customer 360's pending identity challenge must not be silently assumed complete; the verified guest recipient design keeps this feature independent of that program's production verdict.

### Initial measurable service targets

These are design targets to validate against representative staging load, not results already measured. Tighten or revise them with recorded evidence before rollout.

| Measure | Target and verification |
|---|---|
| Transition integrity | All covered stock mutations emit one ordered transition when sellability changes; concurrency tests show zero missed or duplicate episode boundaries for a zero → positive → zero sequence. Daily reconciliation reports zero unexplained sequence gaps and zero persistent drift. |
| Projection freshness | Under normal worker operation, 95% of committed transitions project within 60 seconds and 99% within 5 minutes; alert dispatch never trusts projection without a final ATS check. Record oldest unprocessed transition age and alert on sustained breach. |
| Request API | At representative traffic, 95th-percentile accepted/rejected request latency below 500 ms excluding verification-provider delivery; no duplicate active request under concurrent retry tests. Measure rate-limit outcomes separately. |
| Delivery | At most one restock notification intent per interest/episode/channel; every eligible intent reaches a terminal or actionable retry/unknown state. Alert when queued age exceeds 10 minutes past stabilization or provider failure/unknown-outcome rate exceeds a configured operational threshold. |
| Data protection | Automated tests find no contact value in public DTOs, event payloads, analytics, URLs, logs or unauthorized Admin responses; privacy export/erasure and cross-organization probes pass. |
| Stock and SEO | No alert sends when final ATS is zero; sampled PDP button, API sellability, Product Offer JSON-LD and search projection agree after convergence, with lag visible to operators. |

### Explicit acceptance criteria

- A shopper can request an alert for the exact sold-out variant in a short accessible flow, and can cancel it; no purchase action succeeds without authoritative stock.
- One accepted request is counted once per variant and episode despite retries; all repeated stockout periods are explainable in Product detail.
- Staff can prioritize replenishment from demand without contact permission; authorized staff can review masked contact records and delivery outcomes.
- A stable restock emits at most one permitted alert per request; unavailable/reversed stock, withdrawal, suppression, and unknown provider outcomes are handled safely.
- SEO availability matches visible and purchasable state; canonical stockout pages remain crawlable; analytics honors consent and contains no contact PII.
- Tenant, migration, privacy, performance, and responsive/accessibility checks listed above have recorded evidence. Until then, the plan is not an implementation verdict.
