# Commerce Orders & Customers Target

The area is complete when Storefront and Admin order creation share the same
server invariants; order totals, snapshots, stock reservations, payment facts,
fulfillment, delivery, returns, refunds, audit, and timeline remain consistent;
and operators can efficiently search, filter, inspect, create, safely modify,
hold, resume, cancel, and support orders without bypassing domain operations.

Customer completion requires reliable guest identity, conservative duplicate
handling, current contacts and addresses, source history, authoritative
commerce metrics, linked operational history, safe merge/anonymization, strict
organization isolation, and responsive workflows suitable for Bangladesh
phone, COD, and social-channel operations.
