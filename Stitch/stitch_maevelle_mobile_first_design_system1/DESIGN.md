## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-fashion, boutique digital atelier experience tailored for contemporary women in Bangladesh. Rejecting transient micro-trends, neon gamification, and juvenile pinks, the visual language embraces an intentional, editorial aesthetic centered on craftsmanship, drape, and poise. It balances cultural warmth with quiet luxury.

The target demographic consists of discerning urban shoppers seeking contemporary ethnic wear, artisanal fusion silhouettes, and curated lifestyle essentials. The emotional resonance of the UI is serene, trustworthy, and tactile. Micro-interactions are deliberate and unhurried; typography commands respect with editorial distinction; and negative space is treated as an active framing element rather than empty real estate. The visual philosophy blends warm editorial minimalism with subtle tonal layering, allowing photography, fabric textures, and garment movement to dominate the interface.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in botanical berry tones, warm alabaster clays, and grounding deep charcoals, engineered specifically for high readability and color fidelity on varying display types (from high-density OLED to lower-gamut mobile screens).

### Primary & Brand Accents
- **Primary Conversion (#9E2A4B):** Deep Rich Rose / Muted Berry. Engineered to achieve strict WCAG AA contrast (5.2:1+) against pure white text and light backgrounds. Used for high-intent conversion actions (Add to Bag, Checkout), primary badge indicators, and verified touchpoints.
- **Brand Signature Accent (#D4687F):** Warm Dusty Rose. Used sparingly for curated category markers, delicate active tabs, wishlist toggles, and contextual iconography.

### Surfaces & Canvas
- **Background Canvas (#FAF9F8):** Natural Warm Alabaster. A gentle, eye-strain-reducing background that prevents the harsh glare of generic `#FFFFFF` screens.
- **Card Surface (#FFFFFF):** Warm Crisp White. Reserved for elevated product tiles, modal cards, and interactive form groups.
- **Tinted Blush Surface (#FDF7F7 / #FBF2F3):** Micro-tinted blush layers used for secondary card segments, promo banners, and filter drawer containers.

### Neutrals & Text Hierarchy
- **Primary Text (#1A1617):** Deep Charcoal Warm Black. Softened from pure pitch-black to retain warmth while preserving maximum contrast for sustained reading.
- **Secondary Text (#6E6466):** Muted Earth Gray. Calibrated for garment specifications, material disclosures, SKU details, and crossed-out historical pricing.
- **Structural Borders (#E7DFDE & #EFE9E8):** Hairline edge boundaries ensuring visual structure without visual noise.

### Functional Semantics
- **Stock / Trust (#2D6A4F):** Forest Moss. Signals instant in-stock availability, fast Dhaka delivery slots, and secure checkout integrity.
- **Urgency / Low Stock (#B76E2E):** Warm Amber. Subtle scarcity callouts without aggressive danger signaling.

## Typography

The type system pairs the editorial elegance of **Playfair Display** with the functional clarity and open aperture of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Display & Section Titles:** Playfair Display imparts high-fashion prestige to collection lookbooks, seasonal drops, and promotional storytelling.
- **Interface & Commerce Body:** Plus Jakarta Sans delivers crisp legibility across dense product grids, currency pricing formats (৳ BDT), order trackers, and size selectors.
- **Mobile Zoom Prevention:** All standard input fields, form elements, and selection chips strictly enforce a minimum font size of `16px` on mobile screens to prevent automatic iOS WebKit viewport zooming.
- **Localization Resilience:** Plus Jakarta Sans aligns in optical x-height and baseline rhythm with contemporary Bengali typefaces (such as Hind Siliguri and Noto Sans Bengali), ensuring seamless switching between English and Bangla UI copy without layout jumping.

## Layout & Spacing

The architecture operates on an 8pt base grid with a 4pt sub-grid for nuanced component alignment. Mobile ergonomics govern screen real estate:
- **Mobile Grid (< 640px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` (16px) outer canvas margins and `0.75rem` to `1rem` gutters. Product catalog views default to a 2-column asymmetric or balanced grid, optimized for single-thumb scrolling and natural scanning.
- **Tablet Grid (640px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Desktop Grid (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at a max-width container of `1280px` to maintain comfortable eye-tracking lengths for editorial copy and showcase product imagery.

Touch targets across mobile adhere strictly to a `44px` minimum interactive dimension, accommodating physical reach zones for thumb-driven e-commerce navigation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects heavy, artificial drop shadows in favor of ambient luminescence and precise structural lines.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Flat / Base Layer:** Background canvas (`#FAF9F8`) and section dividers rely on color differentiation and `1px` crisp borders (`#EFE9E8`) without elevation.
2. **Elevated Cards & Drawers:** Cards on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) utilize an ultra-diffused, warm-tinted shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(26, 22, 23, 0.04)`.
3. **Floating & Sticky Elements:** Sticky navigation headers and bottom persistent purchase bars use liquid glass layering: `rgba(250, 249, 248, 0.85)` surface opacity combined with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a subtle `1px` lower border (`#E7DFDE`).
4. **Modal Bottom Sheets:** High-depth checkout sheets and size guides rise with `0 -8px 30px -4px rgba(26, 22, 23, 0.08)`, framed by an overhead backdrop scrim set to `rgba(26, 22, 23, 0.45)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary maintains disciplined, architectural curves that evoke modern luxury rather than casual softness:
- **Small Accents & Badges (`rounded-sm`, 6px):** Curated flash badges (e.g., "New In", "Pure Silk"), size chips, and input text fields.
- **Cards & Media Panels (`rounded-md`, 10px–12px):** Product lookbook cards, promo banners, and modal dialogs.
- **Sticky Bars & Bottom Sheets (`rounded-lg`, 16px–20px):** Cart trays, slide-up filter panels, and notification toasts.
- **Pills Avoided:** Full pill shapes (`rounded-full`) are reserved exclusively for circular icon-only buttons (Wishlist heart, Close actions) to prevent the interface from sliding into juvenile styling.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Deep Rich Rose (`#9E2A4B`) solid fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `12px` border-radius, minimum `48px` height for effortless tap confidence. Active/Pressed state transitions subtly to `#872440`.
- **Secondary CTA:** Pure white background, `1.5px` border in `#9E2A4B`, text colored in `#9E2A4B`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Unbordered, text in `#1A1617` with a delicate animated underline (`#D4687F`) on touch-active states.

### Cards & Product Tiles
- Product tiles feature an editorial 3:4 aspect ratio with `12px` rounded corners.
- Framed by a subtle `1px` border (`#EFE9E8`).
- Price displays present current prices in bold Primary Black (`#1A1617`) paired with slashed MSRP in Muted Earth Gray (`#6E6466`). 

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Selected state: Subtle blush background (`#FBF2F3`), border in Brand Signature Accent (`#D4687F`), text in `#9E2A4B`.
- Default state: Pure white background, border in `#E7DFDE`, text in `#6E6466`. Radius fixed at `6px`.

### Inputs & Selection Controls
- Text inputs display a height of `48px`, background of `#FFFFFF`, border of `1px solid #E7DFDE`, and `8px` corner radius. Placeholder text sits in `#6E6466`.
- Focus state shifts the border to `#9E2A4B` with a `1px` subtle focus ring (`rgba(158, 42, 75, 0.15)`).
- Checkboxes and Radios: Custom styled square/circle controls with `#9E2A4B` check fill upon selection; unselected borders sit in `#E7DFDE`.

### Bottom Sticky Purchase Bar
- Persistent bottom mobile container featuring the liquid glass treatment (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), displaying real-time total price on the left and the primary full-width "Add to Bag" button on the right.

### Badges & Status Indicators
- **In Stock:** Subtle background tint (`#E8F2EC`), text in Forest Moss (`#2D6A4F`), `6px` radius.
- **Low Stock:** Subtle background tint (`#FDF5EC`), text in Warm Amber (`#B76E2E`), `6px` radius.
