## Brand & Style
The design system reflects a curated, feminine, and sophisticated lifestyle brand tailored for contemporary South Asian shoppers. Rooted in modern editorial grace, the interface rejects mass-market discount aesthetics and synthetic neon palettes in favor of a boutique-inspired visual identity. It balances tactile warmth with pristine clarity, conveying prestige while remaining effortlessly accessible.

The visual direction combines **Contemporary Minimalist Editorial** with **Tactile Warmth**:
- Generous, intentional negative space that frames accessories and jewelry like gallery pieces.
- High-contrast typography paired with soft berry-blush backdrops.
- Mobile-first ergonomic craftsmanship optimized for one-thumb browsing in bright tropical daylight.
- Reassuring, dependable interaction patterns designed to build trust for local digital payment systems (bKash, Nagad, cards) and cash-on-delivery validation.

## Colors
The palette centers around an intentional spectrum of berry, blush, and warm alabaster tones, avoiding bubblegum or saturated magenta cliches.

- **Primary (`#9E2A4B`)**: Deep Berry Rose. Exclusively anchors conversion drivers, primary CTAs, active checkout states, and key navigational landmarks. Exceeds WCAG 2.1 AA contrast requirements against white (#FFFFFF) with a 6.2:1 ratio.
- **Secondary (`#D85A7F`)**: Soft Maevelle Rose. Serves as interactive indicators, badge borders, seasonal highlights, and secondary decorative accents.
- **Tertiary / Tint (`#FFF4F6`)**: Delicate Blush Wash. Deployed for subtle state highlights, promotion badges, and low-contrast icon containers.
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF8F6`)**: Warm Linen Neutral. Shields the eye from glare while lending skin-tone warmth to product previews.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure warm-white for cards, drawers, dialogs, and sheets to provide clear separation from the canvas.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#EFE8E6`)**: Architectural hairline borders that preserve layout structure without visual noise.
- **Text & Contrast Hierarchy**:
  - `Text-Primary` (`#1C1917`): Deep warm espresso for maximum legibility in outdoor sun.
  - `Text-Secondary` (`#665F5C`): Muted stone for metadata, subtitles, and input labels.
  - `Text-Tertiary` (`#99908C`): De-emphasized timestamps, breadcrumbs, and inactive counters.
  - `Feedback-Success` (`#1D7044`): Localized verification and in-stock badges.
  - `Feedback-Alert` (`#B42318`): Error validations and stock scarcity warnings.

## Typography
The system uses an intentional dual-type hierarchy to balance editorial polish with strict commerce efficiency:

- **Display & Editorial Accent (`Playfair Display`)**: Used sparingly for collection titles, seasonal showcase headers, and editorial lookbook narratives. It introduces classical beauty without compromising scan speed.
- **Functional Core (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Powers product naming, catalog taxonomy, system prompts, data inputs, and the price display architecture. Its open apertures and balanced proportions offer high legibility on lower-resolution mobile screens and render the Bangladeshi Taka symbol (`৳`) cleanly beside western numerals.
- **Price Displays**: Must always maintain numeric tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure instant eye scanning across compact two-column product grids.

## Layout & Spacing
Designed from the palm outward, the storefront prioritizes thumb-reach zones and tight visual grouping suited for fashion browsing on mobile displays.

- **Mobile Viewports (Under 640px)**:
  - Margin: `16px` (1rem) screen boundary inset.
  - Gutter: `12px` (0.75rem) internal card spacing.
  - Grid: 2-column uniform product distribution. Product cards maintain a strict `3:4` vertical aspect ratio for lifestyle model imagery.
- **Tablet & Wide Breakpoints (641px - 1024px / 1025px+)**:
  - Tablet reflows to 3 columns with `16px` gutters. Desktop scales to a centered 4-column layout constrained to a max canvas width of `1240px` with `24px` gutters.
- **Vertical Rhythm**:
  - Micro-gaps between label and value: `space-xs` (4px).
  - Component-internal content grouping: `space-sm` (8px).
  - Separation between input blocks and interactive clusters: `space-md` (12px) to `space-lg` (16px).
  - Distinct section padding: `space-xl` (24px) to 32px.
- **Touch Target Safeguard**: Any interactive target—including close icons, swatch selectors, and quantity adjusters—must enforce an envelope of at least `48px x 48px`, regardless of the optical icon size.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is communicated through surface tone shifts and warm, diffused drop-shadows rather than stark black shadows or thick graphic lines.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas)**: `#FAF8F6` background. Zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Product Tiles)**: `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a hairline border (`1px solid #EFE8E6`) and an ambient, warm-tinted shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(158, 42, 75, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Action Bars & Micro Dropdowns)**: Elevated panels, bottom sheets, and the persistent sticky purchase bar use `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(158, 42, 75, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Cart Overlays)**: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.16)`. Accompanied by a tinted backdrop scrim: `rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.45)`.
- **Frosted Glass (Backdrop Filter)**: Strictly reserved for two navigational elements to preserve device rendering performance:
  - Top Global Header: `rgba(250, 248, 246, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
  - Bottom Sticky Quick-Checkout Bar: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle `1px` top boundary in `#EFE8E6`.

## Shapes
The shape language uses moderate, tailored curvature that feels modern and poised without turning overly bubbly or toy-like.

- **Base Corner Radius (8px / `rounded-md`)**: Standard across all core visual containers: product cards, photo carousel masks, text input boxes, dropdown selectors, and modal action buttons.
- **Small Accent Radius (4px / `rounded-sm`)**: Used for micro-elements: quantity counters, toast alerts, discount code copy-chips, and secondary variant squares.
- **Large Surface Radius (12px to 16px / `rounded-lg`)**: Applied to top corners of bottom drawers, slide-in filter panels, and delivery verification sheets.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`)**: Exclusively reserved for status badges ("New In", "Low Stock", "Handmade"), notification pips, and thumb-friendly color/size swatch pills.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Touchpoints
- **Primary Action (CTA)**: Height of `48px`, background in Deep Berry (`#9E2A4B`), text in pure white (`#FFFFFF`) at `label-md` weight. Flat state with a subtle active press compression (`transform: scale(0.98)`). Hover state darkens gently to `#8B2340`.
- **Secondary / Outline**: Height of `48px`, white or canvas background, `1px solid #9E2A4B`, text colored `#9E2A4B`. Used for "Add to Bag" on listings or "Save for Later".
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Transparent base, text in `#665F5C`, shifts to `#9E2A4B` on focus/press. Used for quick navigation links and policy disclaimers.

### Chips & Variant Pickers
- **Color & Material Swatches**: Circular (`32px` diameter) with an outer `2px` spacing ring when selected. Disabled variants display a clean diagonal hairline slash across the surface.
- **Size & Style Chips**: Minimum `40px` tap width, `8px` corner radius, styled with `#FAF8F6` fill and a `#EFE8E6` border. Active selection transitions to a `#FFF4F6` background with a `#9E2A4B` border and primary berry text.

### Product Card (Listing Grid)
- **Aspect Ratio**: Visual asset fixed at `3:4` with `overflow: hidden` and `8px` top/bottom corner radii.
- **Content Hierarchy**:
  - Image container with top-right quick-wishlist heart icon (subtle white blur circle, `36px` target).
  - Top-left micro-pill: subtle `#FFF4F6` badge with `#9E2A4B` label for collection callouts.
  - Sub-card layout: Product title (`body-sm`, max 2 lines truncated), followed immediately by price grouping (`price-md` in `#1C1917`, paired with strikethrough original price in `#99908C` if discounted).

### Input Fields & Checkout Selectors
- **Fields**: Minimum height of `48px`, rounded `8px`, crisp `#EFE8E6` border, `#FFFFFF` interior. Placeholder in `#99908C`. Focus shifts border to `#9E2A4B` with an ambient `3px` focus ring tinted at `12%` opacity.
- **Payment Method Selectors**: Custom card containers featuring instant visual logos for mobile wallets (bKash, Nagad) alongside cash-on-delivery. Selected state triggers a `#9E2A4B` border and subtle blush background tint.

### Sticky Bottom Purchase Bar (PDP)
- Fixed to mobile viewport base with safe-area padding (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`).
- Contains real-time product price display on the left (`price-lg`) and a prominent "Add to Bag" or "Buy Now" button spanning the right half (`min-height: 48px`).
- Surface styled in frosted translucent white (`rgba(255,255,255,0.92)`) with a top divider border in `#EFE8E6`.
