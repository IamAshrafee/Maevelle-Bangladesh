How to Measure

4 Key Points
Maevelle Atelier Relaxed Pattern Mapping
1
Bust / Chest

Measure around the fullest part of the bust, keeping the measuring tape horizontal under your armpits.

2
Body Length

From the highest shoulder point straight down over the apex to the bottom hemline.

3
Shoulder Width

From the tip of one shoulder seam straight across your upper back to the opposite shoulder tip.

4
Sleeve Length

From the outer shoulder stitch straight down along the arm to the outer edge of the cuff.
